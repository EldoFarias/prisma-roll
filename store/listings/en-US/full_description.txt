Slide your finger and steer the ball along tracks floating in the sky. Dodge blocks, hammers and spinning bars, jump gaps with speed pads and launchers, and reach the finish line without falling! Prisma Roll is a fast, beautiful and addictive 3D game: easy to start, hard to put down.

★ HOW TO PLAY
• Touch to roll and slide left and right to steer
• Don't fall off and don't hit the obstacles
• Pass by a hair for bonus points in slow motion

• New obstacles in every world: ramps, gaps, narrow bridges, stepping stones, sliding blocks, hammers, spinning bars and rising walls
• Speed pads and jump pads to fly along the track
• Coins along the way

★ ENDLESS MODE
